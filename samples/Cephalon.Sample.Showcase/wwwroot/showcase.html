gRPC Transport

gRPC uses HTTP/2 with binary Protocol Buffers and cannot be tested directly from the browser without gRPC-Web middleware.

Behaviors configured for gRPC transport:

BehaviorPatternProto Service
catalog.get-productDirectCatalogService
catalog.list-productsDirectCatalogService
catalog.create-productDirectCatalogService
catalog.update-productDirectCatalogService
shipping.initiateProcess ManagerShippingService
shipping.trackProcess ManagerShippingService
shipping.confirm-deliveryProcess ManagerShippingService

Test with grpcurl or a native gRPC client:

grpcurl -plaintext localhost:5199 list
grpcurl -plaintext -d '{"name":"test"}' localhost:5199 discovery.DiscoveryService/SayHello

To enable browser testing, add Grpc.AspNetCore.Web and configure gRPC-Web middleware.
